Thakurgaon Sugar Mills Agro-Solar Park

Raised solar earns long-term power income while certified seed, dairy, halal mozzarella, compost and a green multi-crop cold store run on the same ~2,800-acre estate beside Thakurgaon Road station. Phase 4 can scale solar to 500 MW.
